🏗️ Customer & Product 360 Platform

🔗 Live Demo ⌥ GitHub

A self-service analytics platform that unifies a Customer 360 view (account demographics, segmentation, contract terms, health) with a Product 360 view (feature adoption, plan utilization, consumption trends) across 10,000+ business accounts into one browsable surface.

Before this platform, pricing, product, and CS teams each worked from their own data. Usage lived in one tool, MRR in another, segmentation in a spreadsheet. No single place to see a customer's health, utilization, or expansion potential. The Customer 360 view stitches together account demographics, segmentation, contract terms, and health scores into one record per account. The Product 360 view exposes feature adoption, plan utilization, and consumption trends down to the SKU and pricing dimension. A sales rep can open the app, find an account, and instantly see which products that customer is consuming at 140% of their plan limit, then walk into a renewal with the right expansion pitch and no analyst request needed. Owned the product roadmap and 0→1 delivery across engineering, analytics, and business stakeholders. Also owned the end-to-end data architecture: source ingestion, dbt models, the canonical customer/product semantic layer, and the serving layer that keeps every downstream consumer (Streamlit app, Tableau, Salesforce) consistent.

🧩 Framework: Canonical 360 Semantic Layer — one customer/product source of truth feeding every downstream tool
💡 Surfaced a $3M+ MRR recovery opportunity (unmonetized usage above plan limits)  ·  Contributed to a 24% YoY MRR increase  ·  Self-serve adoption across pricing, product, and CS

📈 Customer Analytics & Lifecycle

🔗 Live Demo ⌥ GitHub

Owned KPI development and revenue analytics for a self-serve PAYGO (pay-as-you-go) segment that made up roughly 90% of the customer base. Built revenue attribution frameworks tracking acquisition, conversion, retention, and churn cohorts. Informed product decisions contributing to a 24% YoY MRR increase.

The linked demo is a public, portfolio-shaped version of the same craft: a partner-reporting product for a usage-based PayGo customer base, covering the entire revenue lifecycle (new, expansion, contraction, churn, tier conversion), a PayGo to Enterprise graduation funnel with time-to-convert, churn-reason diagnostics, cohort retention, and per-account drilldowns, all tied to one canonical metric layer. The dashboard splits MRR into plan vs usage (subscription proceeds vs consumption), runs an MRR bridge that ties out every month, and isolates tier conversion from organic expansion so NRR isn't artificially inflated by self-serve to Enterprise jumps. A cohort retention heatmap shows signup-month cohorts across months since signup so PMs can spot structural drop-offs. Native PPTX export on the Overview and Account 360 pages produces a branded executive deck and per-account report in one click. Every metric has a definition tooltip, every page reads from a single semantic layer (lib/metrics.py), and every dollar movement is named and accounted for.

🧩 Framework: MRR Bridge + Plan-vs-Usage model — every dollar of movement named and tied out month over month
💡 ~90% of the customer base · Contributed to 24% YoY MRR increase · End-to-end revenue lifecycle: acquisition, conversion, retention, churn

🧠 Multi-Domain AI Analytics Skill System

🔗 Live Demo ⌥ GitHub

A governed analytics assistant that routes plain-English business questions across 5 domains (Product, Sales, Marketing, HR, Support) and returns structured, metric-grounded answers with an evaluation layer that shows exactly what was checked and how.

Analysts were spending 3–4 hours per week answering the same ad hoc questions. This system eliminates that. A user types any question; a keyword-based domain router classifies it, loads the governed YAML skill file for that domain, and passes only the relevant context to an LLM — so it cannot invent metrics or figures not already defined. Each domain skill encodes its own KPI definitions, formulas, owners, and business logic in structured YAML, fully separate from application code. A new domain is a new YAML file, not a code change. Every answer is evaluated across four dimensions — numeric grounding (deterministic), metric recognition (deterministic), relevance (heuristic), and unsupported-claim detection (heuristic) — with method labels visible in the UI. Owned end-to-end: canonical KPI taxonomy, per-domain YAML skill schema, routing and retrieval layer, and the evaluation harness that keeps answers trustworthy as new domains and metrics are added.

🧩 Framework: Governed Metric Layer + Routing + Eval Harness — domain knowledge in YAML, not hardcoded in prompts; add a domain without touching application code
💡 ~$216K annual productivity value (≈3–4 analyst hrs/week reclaimed × loaded cost)  ·  5 domains live  ·  81% baseline routing accuracy  ·  17 unit tests  ·  Failures shown, not hidden
